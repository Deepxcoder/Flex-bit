📖 FlexBit AI (v2.5.0) — Help Center & Documentation

Welcome! FlexBit AI is an intelligent, 100% offline desktop workspace for renaming, organizing, and searching files.

⚡ Quick Start Checklist

1️⃣ First Launch Setup

FlexBit AI automatically checks for the local vision model (Moondream 2). If missing, it downloads it directly into your local ./models/ folder.

2️⃣ Select a Directory

Use the interactive dropzone on the Home Dashboard to drag & drop any folder or browse your drive.

3️⃣ Pick an Action

Choose whether you want to Rename Photos, Organize Files, or Clean Junk.

4️⃣ Review Previews & Undo

Inspect pre-execution preview tables, select specific files, and click Start. Revert any batch operation with 1 click anytime!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is my data uploaded to any cloud server?

No. FlexBit AI operates 100% offline. All AI inference runs locally on your PC via llama.cpp/Moondream 2. Your files, images, and search history never leave your computer.

Q: How do I move FlexBit AI to a USB drive?

FlexBit AI is completely portable. Simply copy the FlexBit_AI folder (along with its local ./models/ directory) to any external drive. Plug it into any Windows PC and double-click FlexBit_AI.exe to run instantly.

Q: What should I do if a batch rename made a mistake?

Don't worry! Click Recent Sessions in the left sidebar, locate the session, and click Revert Session. All modified files will instantly return to their original names and locations.

Q: Can I use an external AI server instead of local CPU processing?

Yes! In the system settings menu, you can connect FlexBit AI to a local LM Studio or Ollama server instance by providing your local endpoint (http://localhost:1234/v1).
